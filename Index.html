About Me

My name is Ramadan Osama Swedik, and I was born and raised in Benghazi, Libya, where I live with my parents, three sisters, and my twin brother. My father is an accountant, and my mother, formerly an engineer, is now a housewife.

I speak Arabic and English fluently, and I am currently learning Spanish independently. I studied at Thebes International School in Egypt for elementary level, and completed high school at Babil Gardens in Libya. My favorite subjects were Arabic, Chemistry, Biology, and Islamic Education. I found History, Geography, and Civic Education less enjoyable—despite having a genuine interest in history and geography when learning is self-directed.

Although I have not held a formal job yet, I have actively participated in numerous charity events and helped organize various university activities. One of my personal goals is to continue improving my Spanish until I can hold a basic conversation confidently.

In my free time, I enjoy watching documentaries, television shows, and following professional football, especially the progress of FC Barcelona, a team I greatly admire. I also go to the gym regularly, which is one of my favorite hobbies, and I play a variety of games—from board games to video games, including horror, survival, fun, and battleground genres.

Thank you for taking the time to learn a little about me.
